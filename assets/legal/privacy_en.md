# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how {appName} (the "App"), provided by {author}, collects, uses and protects your personal data.

## 1. Data we collect

- Account data: name, email address, profile photo and a unique user ID supplied by your sign-in provider (Google, Microsoft or email and password).
- Authentication data: Firebase Authentication stores your credentials. We never see or store your password in plain text.
- Technical data: app version, device type and crash reports, used to keep the App stable and secure.

## 2. How we use your data

- To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
- To send account-related messages, such as password reset emails.
- To diagnose crashes and improve the App.

We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for advertising.

## 3. Service providers

The App runs on Google Firebase. Firebase processes data on our behalf under its own terms and may store it outside your country. We only share data with providers needed to operate the App.

## 4. Legal basis (EEA and UK users)

We process data to perform our contract with you (providing your account) and for our legitimate interest in security and stability of the App.

## 5. Retention

We keep account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your data within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep some of it.

## 8. Children
